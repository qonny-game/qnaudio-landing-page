Key Features

1. Flexible Pitch & Speed Control

"I want to hear this phrase at half speed right now"—instantly possible with a single tap.

Slow down playback speed while maintaining natural pitch so every single note in blistering fast runs becomes crystal clear. When original keys don't match your vocal range, transpose seamlessly by semitones. Match the song to your vocal range or playing speed effortlessly.

2. Intuitive A-B Looping

Loop those tricky 4 bars 100 or 200 times until it's second nature.

Simply drag across the waveform to set custom loop ranges instantly. No need to rewind from the beginning of the track every time. Zero in on awkward fingerings or vocal slips and repeat them relentlessly to lock them in.

3. Marker Memos for Cues & Notes

"Shallow breath here," "Pitch bends up a half-step"—etch your insights directly onto the track.

Pre-choruses, solo entrances, rhythmic hits. Drop pins on the waveform and jot down notes on the spot. When you open your session next time, your past self's notes guide your practice directly to peak efficiency.

4. Unified Library Management

Your imported tracks remain right where you left them, even after closing the browser.

Say goodbye to the frustration of re-importing tracks from scratch. Save your active practice songs in one place and resume right where you left off. Today's progress waits for you exactly as you left it.

5. Intuitive Keyboard Shortcuts

Never waste a second reaching back for your mouse.

Keep your hands on your instrument or stand right in front of your mic. Control playback, looping, and marker navigation entirely via keyboard shortcuts without breaking your flow. Keep your hands on your instrument and your mind on the music.

6. No Install & Cloud Sync

Your home setup and studio rig unite into a single seamless practice room.

Zero installation required—just open your browser and you're ready. Log in with your Google account to instantly sync markers and notes created on your home PC over to your studio tablet. Practice anywhere, anytime in your personal environment.
